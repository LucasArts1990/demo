    WHALES

    Plant technology into four companies, register the shares you think will pay, and race to 20 points. Every opponent plays the strong engine.

    Players

    How to play

    Four companies, 40 cards, a shared market. The companies are scored as poker hands; you score the shares you kept.

    The deck

    Ranks 1–10 in four suits, each one a shade and a shape: black triangle, dark star, light circle, white square. The suit only matters for a flush or a straight flush.

    Each company holds ranks 1–10 once; so does each shade.

    Each turn — two cards

    1. Field. The round's lead plays one card face up to the market. Everyone else plays one to a company — only one of those holding the fewest cards. Once the market is full the lead plays to a company too.
    2. Register. Play one card in front of you. Its letter is the share you own.

    The lead passes clockwise each round. Cards planted in a company after the opening one are face down; you can always see the ones you planted.

    At five players one market card comes off the deck and everyone registers a share before round one.

    Scoring

    Every card turns face up. Each company makes its best five from its own cards plus the market. Ranking, high to low: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card.

    Same hand → the higher top card wins. Still equal → the earlier letter wins, A ▸ B ▸ C ▸ D.

    Share price by place: 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1, 4th −1. Score each share at its company's price. First to 20 points over repeated games wins the match.

    Reading a card

    The rank sits in the top-left corner, the company colour runs down the right edge. On the table, overlap to the right to read the numbers and to the left to read the companies.
