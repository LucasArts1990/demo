📖 How to play

Tower of Nine Lives · 2–4 players · 15–30 min · ages 10+
Stack cat cards into towers over 9 rounds. Score ★ from your towers and points from three turf wars. Highest total wins.

Components

  • 54 cat cards in 6 races
  • 22 roof cards (18 kinds)
  • 14 hero cards — 7 Paw Alliance, 7 Claw Horde

Setup

  1. Shuffle the cat cards and reveal 5 as the market.
  2. Roofs: put Silver Bell Palace ×(players−1) out as a shared pile. Shuffle the special roofs and reveal (players+1).
  3. Shuffle the heroes and deal 3 to each player, face down. Keep them secret.
  4. Pick a start player.

A cat card

  • Top half — flags. Blue = Paw Alliance, red = Claw Horde. Flags only count in turf wars while they are visible.
  • Bottom strip — race icons and ★. These always count, even when the card is covered.

Your turn

  1. Take 1 card from the market (or from your Treat Stash). Refill the market from the deck.
  2. Place it: start a new tower, or put it on top of one of your open towers. The new card covers the top half of the card below — its flags are hidden, but its icons and ★ stay.
  3. Roof (optional, free): crown one of your towers with a roof from the roof row. The roof hides the top card's flags and closes the tower — nothing more can be added to it.

Limits: at most 4 towers and 5 floors per tower.

Turf wars — end of rounds 3, 6 and 9

  • Everyone secretly chooses 1 hero, then all reveal together. A hero adds 1 flag of its faction.
  • Blue and red are compared separately: visible flags + heroes.
  • Each faction pays 1st / 2nd: round 3 4 / 2, round 6 6 / 3, round 9 8 / 4.
  • Tie for 1st: all tied players get 2nd-place points. Tie for 2nd: no points.
  • The player who scored most in the war starts the next round (tie: the tied player closest to the previous start player, in turn order).
  • Played heroes are discarded.

Game end

After the turf war of round 9, add up war points and every ★ from your cards and roofs (plus Hall of Legends). The highest total wins.

In this app

  • During play you only see war points; tower ★ are revealed card by card at the end.
  • Hover any card to read what it does.
  • After placing a card, click it again to end your turn, or pick a roof first.
